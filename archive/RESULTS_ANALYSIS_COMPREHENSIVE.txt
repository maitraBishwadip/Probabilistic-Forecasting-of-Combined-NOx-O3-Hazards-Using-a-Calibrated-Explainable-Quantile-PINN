[CHALLENGES & CAVEATS]:
  > Imbalanced data: Only 14% positive events (145/1053)
  > Rare regimes: Monsoon & Ventilated-Stormy have only 14 events each
  > Single-pollutant baseline is surprisingly strong (PR_AUC=0.588)
    => Suggests O3 alone may be near-sufficient for prediction
  > Lead time drops significantly 24h to 72h: 24h lead time most reliable

[RECOMMENDATIONS]:
  1. Deploy GBT for operational forecasting (best balanced performance)
  2. Use 24h lead time forecasts (higher skill, degradation by 72h)
  3. Apply regime-specific calibration (performance varies 0.15-0.60 across regimes)
  4. Investigate why single-pollutant baseline is so strong
  5. Collect more data for Monsoon/Ventilated-Stormy regimes (n=14 too small)
